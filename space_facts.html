Did you Know?

  • A sunset on Mars looks blue.

    • Mars has dusty air that makes sunsets look bluish instead of orange-red like Earth.

  • The Moon has no weather.

    • There is no wind or rain, so rocks and footprints can stay there for a very long time.

  • Neptune has the fastest winds in the Solar System.

    • Its winds can blow faster than a jet plane.

  • Earth has a magnetic shield.

    • It helps protect us from dangerous particles coming from the Sun.

  • You would weigh less on the Moon.

    • If you weighed 30 kg on Earth, you would weigh about 5 kg on the Moon because the Moon has weaker gravity.
